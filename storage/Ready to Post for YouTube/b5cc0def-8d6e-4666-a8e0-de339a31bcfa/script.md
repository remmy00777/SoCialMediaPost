How do you turn a trend into original value? Use this three-question framework.

First, define the actual decision. A popular video can show demand, but it does not automatically prove the conclusion.

Second, separate evidence from interpretation. Check what is known, what is missing, and what assumptions are being made.

Third, choose one useful action that fits your audience, your values, and the facts available now.

That is how we turn a trend about software buying decisions into original value instead of a copy. Which of the three questions matters most in your situation?
